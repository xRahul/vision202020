Vision 20-20-20 is an open-source, offline, privacy-first Android eye wellness companion that helps you adhere to the ophthalmologist-recommended 20-20-20 rule: Every 20 minutes of screen time, look at an object 20 feet away for at least 20 seconds.

Extended screen reading reduces human blink rates by more than 60%, causing tear film breakdown and chronic ciliary muscle spasms (Computer Vision Syndrome). Vision 20-20-20 enforces regular rest intervals to keep your eyes lubricated and comfortable.

Key Features:
- Zero Screen Clutter: Unlike intrusive floating widgets, nothing is drawn on top of your screen during the 20-minute working session.
- 20-Second Guided Relaxation: A soothing dark breathing ring guides you to relax your eyes for 20 seconds, then dismisses itself automatically.
- Mobile Reading Mode: Automatically tracks screen-on time and pauses when your screen is locked. A screen-off period of 5+ minutes is recognized as a natural break, resetting the timer.
- Laptop / Desk Companion: Runs continuous wall-clock intervals while sitting beside your computer.
- Lock-Screen Rest Alerts: Wakes sleeping displays with relaxation guidance Google Clock style.
- Quick Settings Tile & Home Widget: Single-tap toggle directly from your notification shade or home screen.
- 100% Offline & Private: No internet permission, no telemetry, no tracking. Free software under GPL-3.0.

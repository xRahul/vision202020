Extended screen reading reduces your natural blink rate from ~18 blinks/minute down to ~5 blinks/minute. This causes dry spots on the cornea and forces the ciliary eye muscles into sustained accommodation spasms (Digital Eye Strain / Computer Vision Syndrome).

Vision 20-20-20 is a free, open-source, offline Android companion that helps you practice the ophthalmologist-backed 20-20-20 Rule:
Every 20 minutes of screen work, gaze at an object 20 feet (6 meters) away for 20 seconds.

WHY VISION 20-20-20 IS DIFFERENT

• Zero Work Clutter: Unlike intrusive floating widgets or floating bubbles, nothing floats over your apps, documents, or games while you work.
• 20-Second Guided Relaxation: When rest time arrives, a calming dark breathing ring guides your eyes to relax, then automatically dismisses itself.
• Screen-Aware Mobile Profile: The timer ticks ONLY while your screen is unlocked. When your screen turns off, the countdown pauses with zero CPU/battery drain.
• Natural Break Auto-Reset: If you put your phone away for 5 or more minutes, the app recognizes you took a natural break and resets the 20-minute cycle automatically.
• Battery Reliability Settings: Opens the system battery-optimization screen so timers survive aggressive OEM power savers.
• Battery-Efficient Themes: Includes pure OLED Black (turns off pixels on OLED screens), Dark Slate, and Light themes.
• Quick Settings Tile & Glance Widget: Single-tap toggle directly from your notification shade or home screen.

100% PRIVATE & OFFLINE

• Zero Internet Permissions: The "android.permission.INTERNET" permission is completely absent from the APK manifest. The app cannot connect to the internet even if it wanted to.
• No analytics, no telemetry, no tracking, no ads, no user accounts.

TRANSPARENT PERMISSIONS BREAKDOWN

• POST_NOTIFICATIONS: To display the ongoing countdown ticker and notify you when rest time begins.
• FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE: Keeps timer tracking accurate without being killed by Android's background app optimizers.
• SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW: (Optional) To display the 20-second calming rest overlay over active apps.
• USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT & SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM: Wakes the display for eye rest Google Clock-alarm style even if your device is locked.
• RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Automatically resumes your eye rest timer after your phone reboots.
• VIBRATE: Plays a subtle tactile cue when rest begins and ends.
